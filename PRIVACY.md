# Alibasa Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

## What Alibasa does

Alibasa translates web pages and selected text using Bing/Microsoft or Google. It does not require an Alibasa account.

## Information processed

When you start a translation, Alibasa reads eligible text from the current page or the text you select and sends it to the selected translation provider. If automatic translation is enabled, it also processes new or changed text on that page while translation remains active. Text may contain personal or sensitive information if it is present in the content you choose to translate.

Alibasa saves your language, provider, appearance and translation preferences locally in your browser. It keeps a bounded, temporary translation cache in extension memory. Translation text is not written to its persistent preferences storage. The cache may be cleared from the main menu and may also be discarded when the extension background process stops.

Alibasa does not include advertising, analytics, browsing-history logging or keystroke logging. It does not request passwords, authentication cookies or payment credentials. Editable form fields are excluded from automatic page translation.

## Translation providers

Translation text and source/target language information are sent over HTTPS directly to Bing/Microsoft or Google. If Bing fails and Google fallback is enabled, the text may also be sent to Google. Those services receive the network information needed to handle requests, including your IP address, and process data under their own policies:

- Microsoft Privacy Statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The extension does not send translation content to a developer-operated server. It does not download or execute code from translation providers.

## Use and sharing

Data is used to provide translation and save your preferences. Alibasa does not sell user data, share it for advertising, use it for unrelated purposes, or use it to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility. Sharing with the translation provider is necessary to provide the translation you request.

## Your choices

You can choose the translation provider, disable Google fallback, stop automatic translation, clear the in-memory cache, restore the original page, or uninstall Alibasa. Uninstalling removes the extension's local preferences. Clearing Alibasa's cache or uninstalling does not delete information already processed by Microsoft or Google; their policies and controls apply to that information.
